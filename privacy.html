Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Sechurplets Studio (“we”, “us”) handles when you visit this website or buy a template.

This website

  • This website does not use accounts, forms, advertising trackers, or analytics, and does not set cookies of its own.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. When your browser loads them, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as with any site that uses hosted fonts. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to operate and secure the service.

The live preview

If you choose a file in the live preview, it is read and processed in your own browser. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. If you load an Excel file, your browser downloads a small file-reading library from the jsDelivr network.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Gumroad. Gumroad collects and processes your payment and contact details under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive the order information Gumroad shares with sellers, such as your name, email, and what you bought, so we can provide support.

The templates

Each template is copied into your own Google account. Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio. When you first run a template, Google asks you to authorize its script, and Google’s terms apply to that permission.

When you contact us

If you email or message us, we use what you send only to reply and to provide support.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email sechurplets.studio@outlook.com.

Changes

If we add analytics or other tools to this site, we will update this page first.
