Know before you buy

What these templates do, and what they don’t.

Google Sheets only

There is no Excel version. You need a free Google account and, ideally, a computer.

You paste in CSV files

The templates do not connect to your store, POS, or contact-center system.

Your data stays in your copy

Data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.

Google asks for permission

On first run Google says it hasn’t verified the app. That is normal for a template you copy yourself, and each START HERE tab walks you through it.

One-time payment

No monthly subscription. Buy a template once and use it from your own Google account.

Try it first

Every template includes sample data and a START HERE tab, so you can see how it behaves before you use your own data.

Real limits, stated up front

Tested sizes are shown on each product page. Results depend on your data and are not guaranteed.

Got questions?

Everything to know before you buy.

Do I need coding skills?

No. Each template comes with its script already installed. Paste your export, click the run button, then review the result tabs. A START HERE tab walks you through it, and sample data lets you try it first.

Does it work in Microsoft Excel?

No. The templates work in Google Sheets only, and there is no Excel version.

Is my data safe?

Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.

Why does Google say it hasn't verified the app?

This is normal for a template you copy yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to [tool name] (unsafe), then Allow. The script works on that spreadsheet only. Every template's START HERE tab repeats these steps.

Does it connect to my store, POS, or contact-center system?

No. You paste in exported files (CSV). Nothing is connected to your accounts.

What if my export layout is different from the sample?

You match your column names once in the template's settings tab. If you are unsure whether a template fits your export, email us before you buy.

Can I share a template with my whole team?

Each purchase is licensed per business. Email us if you need a multi-seat or agency license. See the Terms & license page for details.

What if something goes wrong with my purchase?

See Refunds & support, or email us with your order details.
