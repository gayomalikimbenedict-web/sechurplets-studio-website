Refunds & Support

We want your template to work on your data. If it does not, tell us.

Getting help

  • Email sechurplets.studio@outlook.com or message us on WhatsApp at +63 997 4653 945.
  • Include the name of the template, your order details, and a description of what went wrong. Please do not send private customer data; a few anonymized rows are enough.
  • Each template has a START HERE tab with the setup steps and sample data. Try that first if something looks wrong.
  • If you are unsure whether a template fits your export, email us before you buy and describe your columns.

Refunds

Templates are digital products delivered right after purchase. If your link does not work, or a template does not run as described on its product page, email us with your order details. We will work with you to fix the problem or discuss a refund.

Payments are processed by Gumroad, and Gumroad’s own policies also apply to your payment.
