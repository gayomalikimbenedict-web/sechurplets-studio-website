What the preview does

The same workflow described on the product page.

  • Duplicate Order IDs move to DUPLICATES_ARCHIVE. The first copy is kept.
  • Cancelled orders are taken out of the fulfillment list and listed for review.
  • Short US zip codes get their leading zero back (2134 becomes 02134).
  • Refund-related and unusual records (missing details, invalid email, zero total) are flagged in REFUNDS_&_RISK_LOG.
  • The status line checks that rows read equal rows accounted for. Nothing is deleted.
  • Export downloads the cleaned list as a fulfillment CSV.

It flags unusual records for you to review. It does not detect fraud and does not fix every possible data problem. If you open the exported CSV in Excel and save it again, Excel may hide leading zeros in zip codes.
