Shopify Payout & Refund Reconciler

Find the orders, refunds, and payouts that do not add up.

A Google Sheets template that compares your exported orders, refunds, and payout transactions, then shows what matches and what needs a closer look. It points you to the records to check. It does not fix them.

What’s included
  • The reconciliation template with its script already installed.
  • A START HERE tab and sample data.
  • Result tabs: SUMMARY, MATCHED, UNMATCHED, DISCREPANCIES, and DUPLICATES.
  • A run log and an adjustable tolerance for rounding differences.
How it works
  1. Export your orders, refunds, and payout transactions from Shopify as CSV and paste each into its tab.
  2. Match your column names once in CONFIG.
  3. Click Run reconciliation.
  4. Records are matched by order number, and amounts and currencies are compared.
  5. SUMMARY shows the counts and a money-to-review total for each currency. Currencies are never mixed.
  6. Review the unmatched records, amount differences, and duplicates.

Tested size: 20,000 orders per period.

Good to know
  • Money to review shows where to look first. It is not a loss figure.
  • It does not connect to your Shopify account. You paste in exported reports.
Before you buy

How you get it, and what to expect

How you get it

After purchase you receive a link. It opens a Make a copy screen in Google Sheets. The template then lives in your own Google account, and you can use it right away.

First time you open it

Google shows a screen saying ‘Google hasn’t verified this app’. This is normal for a template you copy yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to [tool name] (unsafe), then Allow. The script works on that spreadsheet only. Each template has a START HERE tab with these steps and a sample data button, so you can try it before using your own data.

What you need

A free Google account and Google Sheets. A computer is recommended.

What it does not do

  • It works in Google Sheets only, with no Excel version.
  • It does not connect to your store, POS, or contact-center system. You paste in exported files (CSV).
  • Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.
  • Tested sizes are shown on each page. Results depend on your data and are not guaranteed.
FAQ
Does it handle multiple currencies?

Yes. Amounts are compared only within the same currency, and a currency mismatch is flagged.

Can it ignore small rounding differences?

Yes. Set a tolerance, for example 0.01, in CONFIG.

Does it fix the problems it finds?

No. It lists them so you can fix them in Shopify or your books.
