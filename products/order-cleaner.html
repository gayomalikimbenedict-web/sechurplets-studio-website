Fulfillment-Ready Order Cleaner

Fix messy order exports before they become wrong shipments.

A Google Sheets template that turns raw order exports (CSV) from your store or POS into one clean, fulfillment-ready list. Duplicates, cancelled orders, and refund records are sorted out for you, and nothing is deleted.

What’s included
  • The Order Cleaner template with its script already installed.
  • A START HERE tab and sample data so you can try it first.
  • Three result tabs: CLEANED_MASTER, DUPLICATES_ARCHIVE, and REFUNDS_&_RISK_LOG.
  • A one-click fulfillment CSV export, and a run log.
How it works
  1. Paste your order export into the IMPORT tab and match your column names once in CONFIG.
  2. Click Run full clean.
  3. Duplicate Order IDs move to an archive. The first copy is kept.
  4. Cancelled orders are taken out of your fulfillment list and listed for review.
  5. Short US zip codes get their leading zero back (for example 2134 becomes 02134).
  6. Refund-related records and unusual ones (missing details, invalid email, zero total) are flagged in the risk log.
  7. Export the fulfillment CSV, then clear the sheet for the next batch.

Tested size: 50,000 orders per batch.

Good to know
  • It flags unusual records for you to review. It does not detect fraud and does not fix every possible data problem.
  • If you open the exported CSV in Excel and save it again, Excel may hide leading zeros in zip codes. Upload the CSV directly to your system instead.
Before you buy

How you get it, and what to expect

How you get it

After purchase you receive a link. It opens a Make a copy screen in Google Sheets. The template then lives in your own Google account, and you can use it right away.

First time you open it

Google shows a screen saying ‘Google hasn’t verified this app’. This is normal for a template you copy yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to [tool name] (unsafe), then Allow. The script works on that spreadsheet only. Each template has a START HERE tab with these steps and a sample data button, so you can try it before using your own data.

What you need

A free Google account and Google Sheets. A computer is recommended.

What it does not do

  • It works in Google Sheets only, with no Excel version.
  • It does not connect to your store, POS, or contact-center system. You paste in exported files (CSV).
  • Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.
  • Tested sizes are shown on each page. Results depend on your data and are not guaranteed.
FAQ
Which exports does it work with?

CSV order exports that include the required columns. You set your column names once in CONFIG.

Does it delete my data?

No. Duplicates and cancelled orders are archived or logged, and your pasted data stays in the IMPORT tab until you clear it.

Can I change what counts as cancelled or refunded?

Yes. These words are settings in CONFIG.
