How it works
  1. Put your data in your own tabs, each with a header row, and list the tab names in CONFIG with a priority number.
  2. Click Build mapping. Similar column names are matched for you, and you can correct any match.
  3. Choose the column that identifies the same record, for example Email.
  4. Click Run merge.
  5. Duplicates are archived. If the winning record has a blank, the value can be filled from a lower-priority duplicate.
  6. Values that differ between sources are listed in CONFLICTS.
  7. SUMMARY proves nothing was lost: rows read equal rows in MASTER plus rows archived.

Tested size: about 90,000 rows in total across all source tabs.

Good to know
  • It combines compatible data and flags what needs review. It does not fix every inconsistency.
  • Merged values are written as text, exactly as they look in your source tabs.
  • Conflicts can only be found when you choose a key column.
Before you buy

How you get it, and what to expect

How you get it

After you order and pay, we send you a link. It opens a Make a copy screen in Google Sheets. The template then lives in your own Google account, and you can use it right away.

First time you open it

Google shows a screen saying ‘Google hasn’t verified this app’. This is normal for a template you copy yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to [tool name] (unsafe), then Allow. The script works on that spreadsheet only. Each template has a START HERE tab with these steps and a sample data button, so you can try it before using your own data.

What you need

A free Google account and Google Sheets. A computer is recommended.

What it does not do

  • It works in Google Sheets only, with no Excel version.
  • It does not connect to your store, POS, or contact-center system. You paste in exported files (CSV).
  • Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.
  • Tested sizes are shown on each page. Results depend on your data and are not guaranteed.
FAQ
Will it change my original tabs?

No. Only the result tabs are rebuilt.

What if two sources disagree?

The higher-priority source wins, and the difference is listed in CONFLICTS.

Can I use CSV files?

Yes. Paste each CSV into its own tab.
