Support Team Scorecards

See how each agent and team is doing, and where to coach, from the logs you already export.

A Google Sheets template that turns call, chat, or ticket logs into a scorecard for every agent and a summary for every team. You set the thresholds, and anything outside them is highlighted.

One-time payment. Google Sheets only · no Excel version.

What’s included
  • The Support Team Scorecards template with its script already installed.
  • A START HERE tab and sample data (six invented agents).
  • Result tabs: SCORECARDS, TEAM_SUMMARY, and SUMMARY, plus a TEAMS tab for assigning teams.
  • A run log.
How it works
  1. Paste your activity log into the ACTIVITY tab and match your column names in CONFIG.
  2. Set your own thresholds, for example a maximum average handle time and a minimum resolution rate.
  3. Click Build scorecards.
  4. Each agent gets interactions, interactions by type, average handle time, and resolution rate. Team summaries come from your team tags or the TEAMS tab.
  5. Red cells show where a result falls outside your thresholds, and a Flags column says why.

Tested size: about 99,000 rows in ACTIVITY.

Good to know
  • Scorecards are a reporting aid. They do not judge performance for you, and the thresholds are the ones you set.
  • Agents with very few interactions are not judged on handle time or resolution, so one long call cannot unfairly flag someone.
  • It does not connect to your contact-center platform.
Before you buy

How you get it, and what to expect

How you get it

After purchase you receive a link. It opens a Make a copy screen in Google Sheets. The template then lives in your own Google account, and you can use it right away.

First time you open it

Google shows a screen saying ‘Google hasn’t verified this app’. This is normal for a template you copy yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to [tool name] (unsafe), then Allow. The script works on that spreadsheet only. Each template has a START HERE tab with these steps and a sample data button, so you can try it before using your own data.

What you need

A free Google account and Google Sheets. A computer is recommended.

What it does not do

  • It works in Google Sheets only, with no Excel version.
  • It does not connect to your store, POS, or contact-center system. You paste in exported files (CSV).
  • Your data is processed inside your own copy of the sheet. Nothing is sent to Sechurplets Studio.
  • Tested sizes are shown on each page. Results depend on your data and are not guaranteed.
FAQ
What if my log has start and end times, not a handle time?

It can work out the handle time from them.

What counts as resolved?

You list the values that count, for example resolved, solved, closed.

Can I measure only calls?

Yes. Set the interaction type in CONFIG.
